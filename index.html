Know what you're looking at

One forecast. Two perspectives.

A corrected number is a model estimate, not a measurement or a guarantee.

LIVE INPUTS

Forecasts for your place

The app fetches current GFS rainfall, humidity, pressure and wind. Future daily totals cover midnight to midnight in local time. Lead matching is approximate, not exact model-initialization alignment.

LEARNED CORRECTION

Fitted, not hand-picked

Three lead-specific corrections were fitted on archived forecasts and ERA5 rain reanalysis. Coordinates are model inputs; predictions away from evaluated points remain geographic extrapolation.

VISIBLE LIMITS

Warnings stay unavailable

The live exact-zero ensemble lowers MAE and RMSE versus v1 at every lead in exploratory evaluation. Its 72h RMSE remains worse than raw GFS. Research warning models miss events and create too many false alarms, so no warning or all-clear is issued.

Use official forecasts for safety decisions.

Withholding a warning does not mean an area is safe. ERA5 is model-based reanalysis, not local rain-gauge truth. A daily rainfall total does not establish flood impact.

Good to know

Useful numbers, without false certainty.

Does this work at every Indian location?

Search worldwide or enter coordinates for provider forecasts. Learned rain correction is available only for named Indian locations in the training scope. Coordinate-only locations are provider-only. The rainfall model calculates between its regional training points, but useful skill has not been established at every coordinate. The app displays the nearest evaluated point and extrapolation limits. Its geographic bounds are not an exact political-border check.

Why can a zero forecast become a small amount?

The current regression can produce a small positive estimate even when raw GFS predicts zero. It is statistical uncertainty, not observed rain. Zero-preserving and rain-occurrence alternatives are being tested; forcing zero can also suppress rainfall GFS missed.

Is it learning automatically every day?

Not currently. A guarded retraining pipeline is being prepared. Additional data and retraining do not guarantee improvement; the previous model must be kept when a candidate fails comparison checks.

What is saved on my device?

Your color-theme preference is saved locally. Search terms and selected coordinates go to Open-Meteo to fetch forecasts; the site does not request device location or send alert messages.
